# Evaluation data license policy

This is an engineering gate, not legal advice. Product releases should retain
the registry, dataset lock, attribution output, and local review evidence for
legal review.

- `strict`: only CC0-1.0 and CC-BY-4.0, with commercial use explicitly true.
- `sharealike`: CC-BY-SA-4.0 and an explicit `--allow-sharealike` decision.
- `manual-review`: a valid local approval JSON is additionally required.

Unknown datasets, unknown SPDX identifiers, NC terms, non-commercial or
research-only terms, Article 30-4-only terms, missing source URLs, and false or
missing commercial-use declarations are rejected. Approval files cannot
override a forbidden license.

Common Voice also forbids speaker re-identification and raw-data rehosting or
resharing. Those restrictions remain binding even though its dataset license
is CC0-1.0.
