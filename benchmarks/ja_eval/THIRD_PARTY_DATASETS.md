# Third-party evaluation datasets

No dataset audio or archive is distributed in this repository.

| Dataset | Registry ID | Policy | License | Acquisition |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Common Voice Scripted Speech 26.0 Japanese | `common_voice_ja_26` | strict | CC0-1.0 | Mozilla Data Collective or local archive |
| Google FLEURS Japanese | `fleurs_ja` | strict | CC-BY-4.0 | pinned Hugging Face revision |
| NICT SPREDS-U1 Japanese | `spreds_u1_ja` | strict | CC-BY-4.0 | local archive |
| CPJD | `cpjd` | sharealike | CC-BY-SA-4.0 | local archive |
| JVNV | `jvnv` | sharealike | CC-BY-SA-4.0 | local archive |
| JNV | `jnv` | sharealike | CC-BY-SA-4.0 | local archive |
| ITA-Corpus-Rion | `ita_rion` | manual-review | CC-BY-4.0 plus additional terms | approved local archive |

The machine-readable source URLs, versions, restrictions, and expected counts
are authoritative in `config/datasets.yaml`. Reports derive attribution
requirements from that registry rather than from dataset content.

Common Voice access requires accepting the dataset terms through Mozilla Data
Collective. Do not identify speakers, expose source `client_id` values, re-host,
or reshare the downloaded archive, extracted clips, prepared audio, or complete
local manifests. Repository reports must contain only aggregate results and
opaque sample identifiers.
